What this direction is testing
Client testimony, 2026-08-14 This direction is the one the new evidence hits directly. The room's feedback — surfaced through Women in Rust — is that everything is very intimidating; people aren't allowed to be wrong. Hands up for A, one letter at a time, is public commitment with your name attached to your face. The newcomer who isn't sure raises nothing, visibly, five times running. A's entire pitch rests on public commitment being the good part.

The room votes out loud, option by option. The projector asks for one letter at a time in 150px type; the host counts hands and taps the number. Nobody joins anything.

Stories that evaporate. B1 (join without an account), B5 (capacity, AC-52–55), B7's pre-reveal payload work, and most of B6 — there is no participant client to leak from, no write burst to load-test, and nothing per-person exists to record. The build shrinks to a projector and a host control.

AC-39 does not survive here as written. It wants anonymous per-option totals. Hands produce a count only because the host types it, and a raised hand was never anonymous to the room. A is maximally private in the record and maximally public in the moment — which is exactly the wrong way round for the problem the client just named.

Second strike, found later. AC-48 was rewritten at T-13: the hint belongs on each participant's own device, and taking it must tell nobody. With no participant device, A's only possible hint is announced to the whole room — "we need a hint" said out loud. A cannot satisfy AC-48 as it now stands, and that was invisible until the hint question got asked.

Can it be rescued? Honestly: not without it stopping being Direction A. Eyes-closed voting is the classroom fix and it is a strange thing to ask a room of adults for. Every mitigation removes the public commitment, and the public commitment is the direction. It stays in the fan-out anyway — the client's call at T-14, and the right one: it costs nothing to keep, and in October it is a built comparison for do phones help or hurt rather than a hypothetical. A direction you can watch lose is worth more than one you never built.

AC-91 gets easier, AC-38 gets harder. Everyone reads the same screen at the same instant, so time-to-opinion is one measurement, not fifty. But the projector is the only place the code exists — if the back row can't read it, there is no fallback.
