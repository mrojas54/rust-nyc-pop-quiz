What this direction is testing

This is the expensive direction. It is the only one that needs Stories B1, B5, B6 and B7 in full: a join path, a 200-concurrent deadline write burst (AC-52–55, the highest-risk moment in the system), and canary-secret proof that no pre-reveal payload carries the answer.

What it buys. A real anonymous count rather than the host's thumb on a counter, a per-person "you picked A" computed on-device and never transmitted (AC-58), and code you can hold six inches from your face — which is the only version of AC-32 that a back row can actually pass.
