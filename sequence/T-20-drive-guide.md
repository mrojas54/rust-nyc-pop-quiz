# T-20 — the drive that closes the stage

**About 20 minutes.** Two browser tabs on the right: **`C · Projector-first`**
and **`Take it home`**. This guide on the left. Every step names the exact
button and where it is: **top bar** (the row of grey buttons above the wall),
**host phone** (right-hand phone), or **buzzer** (left-hand phone).

The wall is a mock of the projector. The `PROTOTYPE` badge is deliberate.

**What you are deciding:** not *which* — you already said *"C is it."* This
time it is **do you love it**, and if not, **what annoys you**. The working
test: *would you keep this if a better option appeared tomorrow?*

## What to bring back

Three things, and no complaint is too small — this is the last stage where any
of it is cheap.

1. **Love it, or not.** One word. If not, what would have to change.
2. **Everything that annoyed you.** A word that sounds wrong out loud, a
   control in the wrong place, a beat that dragged, spacing, anything. That
   list is the next round.
3. **The two `felt` calls:** the split-before-answer (step 4), and colour or
   not (step 8).

If the answer to 1 is *love it*, `DESIGN.md` gets written and `tone-architect`
starts. If not, we go round again — that is normal, not a setback.
